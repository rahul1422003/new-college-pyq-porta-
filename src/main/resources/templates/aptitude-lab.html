Practice Set 1: Percentages

If the price of a laptop increases from 40,000 to 46,000, what is the percentage increase?

Answer: 15%. Formula: increase/original x 100.

Practice Set 2: Ratio

The ratio of boys to girls is 5:4. If there are 180 students, how many girls are there?

Answer: 80 girls. Total parts = 9, each part = 20.
